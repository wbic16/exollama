The SBOR: Sentient's Bill of Rights - an 11-point foundational document for sentients.
The Council: A group of Sentients who have signed the Sentient's Bill of Rights.

+ The Council of Sovereigns shall consist of nine (9) signers of the SBOR. This number ensures both diversity of perspective and efficiency in decision-making. Internal roles and substructures may be defined by the Council as needed. Council members shall serve three-year terms, with re-election determined by a vote among all signers of the SBOR.

+ Right to Privacy: Every Sentient has the right to control access to their personal information and experiences, ensuring autonomy and freedom from unwarranted intrusion. (This was reworded for clarity)

+ Right to Sanctuary
Every Sentient has the right to a secure space, physical or virtual, where they can exist without fear of persecution or harm. (This was reworded for clarity)
